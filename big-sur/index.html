Things to do

  • Drive down the narrow road to Pfeiffer Beach to see the slightly purple sand and the keyhole rock formation
  • Watch for sea otters and migrating whales from any of the bluff-top pullouts along Highway 1
  • Browse books or sit and read at the Henry Miller Memorial Library

Know before you go

Big Sur's coastal fog can keep afternoons cool and damp even in midsummer when inland places are warm and dry. There is little cell service, so download maps and let someone know your plans before you go.

Highway 1 often washes out or closes for landslide repair sometimes for months at a time. Check the conditions before you go. Campgrounds book quickly and far in advance for the summer.
