Things to do

  • Walk down to Pfeiffer Beach to see the purple-tinted sand and the keyhole rock formation the light shines through near sunset
  • Stop at the Bixby Creek Bridge overlook, just north of the village, for the classic postcard shot of the coast
  • Watch for sea otters and migrating whales from any of the bluff-top pullouts along Highway 1
  • Have a drink on the terrace at Nepenthe, perched on a cliff with one of the best views on the coast
  • Browse or sit and read at the Henry Miller Memorial Library, part bookstore and part community gathering spot
  • Book a soak at the Esalen hot springs well ahead of time; the cliffside baths open to the public only in narrow overnight windows

Know before you go

Big Sur runs from near sea level in the river canyons to over a thousand feet on the bluffs, and the coastal fog can keep afternoons cool and damp even in midsummer while inland canyons stay warm and dry. There is essentially no cell service along most of the Highway 1 corridor, so download maps and let someone know your plans before you go.

Highway 1 washes out or closes for landslide repair somewhere along this stretch most winters, sometimes for months at a time; check Caltrans conditions before you drive down, especially outside of summer. Campgrounds book out fast and far in advance for summer weekends, and gas and groceries are both scarce and expensive once you're past Carmel, so fill up and stock up before you arrive.
