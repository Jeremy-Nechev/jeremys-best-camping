Things to do

  • Walk the Trail of 100 Giants at sunrise or sunset to have the grove to yourself
  • Visit Noble Young Falls, along the highway
  • Drive the full length of the Western Divide Highway, a scenic byway connecting Highway 190 to Highway 155
  • Fish Freeman Creek below Redwood Meadow
  • Stargaze from any meadow
  • Visit the Natural Tubs https://maps.app.goo.gl/EdWW7LxKUcaHSfDg9

Know before you go

Ponderosa, at the north end of the Western Divide Highway, has gas, and a grocery store. There is very little cell service in the area.

The Western Divide Highway is paved but narrow and winding, with drop-offs in places; take it slow, especially towing a trailer. The road closes with in winter and doesn't reopen until late spring.

Campfire and stove restrictions are common in late summer and vary by year, check the Sequoia National Forest website before you go.
