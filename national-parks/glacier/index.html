Things to do

  • Drive Going-to-the-Sun Road, 50 miles over Logan Pass with pullouts for waterfalls, glaciers, and hanging valleys (vehicle reservation required in summer; book the same day as your campsite)
  • Take a Red Bus tour if you'd rather not drive it yourself, an easy way to see the highlights with kids without anyone hiking
  • Rent a rowboat at Many Glacier or Lake McDonald Lodge
  • Ride the Two Medicine boat shuttle to shorten several trailheads on that side of the park
  • Pick up a Junior Ranger booklet at the Apgar or St. Mary visitor center; both run free ranger-led programs in summer

Know before you go

Entry is $35 per vehicle for seven days, and a separate vehicle reservation is required for the Going-to-the-Sun Road corridor and the Many Glacier and Two Medicine areas from roughly late spring through early fall; book both the moment your travel dates are set. The road over Logan Pass is usually only fully open from mid-June to mid-October, weather depending.

This is serious grizzly and black bear country. Carry bear spray on every hike, know how to use it before you need it, and store all food and scented items in the metal lockers provided at every campground. Weather changes fast at elevation, with snow possible even in July at Logan Pass. Cell service is minimal to nonexistent through most of the park, including at Many Glacier.
