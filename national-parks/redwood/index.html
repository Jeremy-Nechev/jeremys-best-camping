Things to do

  • Drive the Newton B. Drury Scenic Parkway, a slower road through old growth trees
  • Watch for Roosevelt elk in the meadow at Elk Prairie, especially at dawn and dusk
  • Go to the tide pools at Enderts Beach near Crescent City at low tide
  • Stop at the Kuchel Visitor Center for a Junior Ranger booklet and tide charts
  • Go to Gold Bluffs Beach for sunset

Know before you go

Redwood is actually a joint operation between the National Park Service and California State Parks. There isn't any enterance fee for the National park area but the state parks charge a fee for certain parking areas. This is a large park so make sure you plan out your driving.

Certain unpaved roads side roads like the one to Fern Canyon close after heavy rain, so check the conditions before you go.
