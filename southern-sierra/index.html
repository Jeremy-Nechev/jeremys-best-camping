Region

Southern Sierra

Giant sequoias and granite backcountry, tucked in the Sequoia National Forest foothills above Springville. Closer to Los Angeles than Yosemite or the main Sequoia National Park entrance, and far quieter than either.

How to choose

Trail of 100 Giants & Ponderosa is the easier of the two. The grove trail itself is nearly flat, the campgrounds along the Western Divide Highway are a short drive apart, and the store and lodge at Ponderosa mean you can restock or grab a hot meal without leaving the area. It's the better pick for a first trip up here, or if you'd rather not haul every meal in with you.

Mountain Home State Forest is the quieter, more self-sufficient option. There's no store, no gas, and spotty cell service once you're past the entrance, so it rewards people who plan ahead and want a genuinely remote-feeling trip that's still only a few hours from the city. Both sit at similar elevation and both are open the same season, so pick based on how much you want to rough it, not the weather.

Either way, this region is the sequoia fix for people who don't want to compete for a Sequoia National Park reservation or fight Yosemite traffic. Sites here are almost all first-come or lightly booked, and even on a summer weekend you'll see a fraction of the crowds.
