Foil-packet dinner

One pan, no cleanup

Time
30 min
Effort
Easy

Ingredients: sausage or chicken thighs, potatoes, bell pepper, onion, olive oil, salt, pepper.

Steps: Chop everything into bite-size pieces, toss with oil and seasoning, and split between two sheets of heavy-duty foil. Fold into sealed packets and set on hot coals or a grill grate for 20 to 25 minutes, flipping once halfway through.

Dutch oven chili

Feeds a crowd, better the second night

Time
90 min
Effort
Medium

Ingredients: ground beef, onion, garlic, two cans of beans, one can of diced tomatoes, chili powder, cumin, salt.

Steps: Brown the beef and onion in the Dutch oven over the fire, then stir in the garlic and spices. Add the beans and tomatoes, cover, and simmer over low coals for 45 to 60 minutes, stirring every so often so it doesn't stick.

Camp breakfast burritos

Prep at home, reheat on the stove

Time
15 min
Effort
Easy

Ingredients: scrambled eggs, cooked sausage or bacon, shredded cheese, flour tortillas, hot sauce.

Steps: The night before you leave, scramble the eggs, cook the meat, and roll everything with the cheese into tortillas. Wrap each burrito tightly in foil and freeze. At camp, set the foil packets on the stove or a low grate for 10 to 12 minutes, turning once, until heated through.

Skillet cornbread

Goes with everything

Time
40 min
Effort
Medium

Ingredients: a box of cornbread mix (or cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, egg, and milk from scratch), oil for the pan.

Steps: Mix the batter according to the package, or your usual recipe. Heat a well-oiled cast iron skillet over coals until it's good and hot, pour in the batter, and cover loosely with foil. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, rotating the skillet occasionally so it browns evenly.

Trail-mix pancakes

Kids will eat four

Time
20 min
Effort
Easy

Ingredients: pancake mix, water or milk, oil, a handful of trail mix (nuts, dried fruit, chocolate chips).

Steps: Mix the batter per the package directions, then fold in the trail mix. Cook on a greased griddle or skillet over medium coals, flipping once bubbles form on top, about 2 minutes per side.

Grilled peaches

The dessert that makes people talk

Time
10 min
Effort
Easy

Ingredients: ripe peaches, melted butter or oil, honey, vanilla ice cream or yogurt (optional).

Steps: Halve and pit the peaches, brush the cut sides with butter, and grill cut-side down over medium coals for 4 to 5 minutes, until charred and starting to soften. Drizzle with honey and serve warm, with ice cream if you brought a cooler.
