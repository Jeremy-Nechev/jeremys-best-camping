How to choose

Big Bear is the pick if you want pine forest, a real lake, and enough going on in the village to fill a rainy afternoon. It runs cooler than the others, so it's the best summer escape from inland heat, and it's the only one of the four with real winter snow.

San Diego Mountains is mountain camping at Laguna, Cuyamaca, Palomar, and William Heise, with cabins if anyone would rather not sleep in a tent. It's a good choice for a first trip, or for a group that's still deciding how much they like camping.

Anza-Borrego is the desert option, down from the San Diego mountains, for a warm winter weekend when the mountains are cold.

Idyllwild is the smallest-feeling of the four, built around a walkable mountain town, and it's the best option if you want a short hike followed by a real restaurant meal without breaking camp. All four work fine for families; the county park and developed sites in each region have flush toilets and are close enough to town for an easy exit if a first trip doesn't go as planned.
